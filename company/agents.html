How our agents work.

Every account at UQT is led by a senior specialist. The engine scores. The agent steers. Each agent owns their corner of the network: banking, FX, digital assets, onboarding operations. Together they cover every kind of file we route.

You have one point of contact across the entire engagement. Your agent stays with you from first call to live accounts, and through every subsequent introduction.

Banking Director — Adira K.

Adira leads the banking desk. 14 years in corporate banking onboarding, previously running multi-currency and multi-entity programmes at a top-tier global bank. She personally reviews every banking shortlist before introductions go out, calling partners where helpful.

Remit. Multi-currency operating accounts. Multi-entity group banking. High-balance corporate banking. Settlement and treasury rails.

FX & Treasury Lead — Mateus R.

Mateus runs the FX desk. He routes flow to the partners who price each corridor best, builds hedging programmes for clients with material currency exposure, and stays on top of treasury options including yield and money-market access.

Remit. Spot and forwards in 40+ currencies. Corporate hedging programmes. Mass payouts and payroll FX. Emerging-market routing.

Regions. Global.

Digital Assets Counsel — Yara N.

Yara steers digital-asset onboarding through regulated routes only. Background in compliance and crypto-native treasury. She refuses to place clients with unlicensed providers, and will tell a client up front when a regulated route does not exist for their activity.

Remit. Regulated custody partnerships. Fiat on/off-ramp routing. OTC block-trade introductions. Treasury for crypto-native businesses.

Regions. MENA and EU.

Imran is your day-to-day point of contact. He packages each application, manages provider questions, keeps status live in the portal, and unblocks the file when it stalls. The infrastructure works because Imran's job is to make sure it does.
