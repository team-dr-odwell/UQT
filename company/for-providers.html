What partners get.

Banks, FX desks and regulated digital-asset providers in the UQT network receive a stream of pre-qualified, well-matched business applicants. Each introduction is packaged the way your team prefers, with the documents you actually need, from clients whose activity fits your risk appetite.

01

Pre-qualified

Each applicant is scored against your loaded profile. The introductions you receive are the ones likely to onboard.

02

Packaged for your team

Files arrive in the format your onboarding team prefers, with documents and structure questions answered up front.

03

Volume that fits

We do not flood any partner. The pipeline matches your capacity and your risk appetite.

How partnership works.

We meet with your team. We learn what your desk looks for, sector by sector and corridor by corridor. We agree the loaded profile, the volume of introductions you want to see per month, the documents you require, and the typical decision timeframe. The engine then routes accordingly.

You see only introductions that match your loaded profile. Quality, not volume.

Are we a fit?

We work with regulated banks, FX providers, payment institutions, regulated digital-asset providers (VASPs) and selected fintech infrastructure providers. We are selective and the bar is high. We add partners where the experience for clients will be strong.

Joining the network.

  1. Initial conversation with our partnerships team.
  2. Diligence on licence, desk, team and onboarding experience.
  3. Loaded profile session. We capture what your desk looks for.
  4. Soft-launch with a small batch of test introductions.
  5. Full activation and ongoing review.
