The common pattern.

UQT clients share a shape. Cross-border. Multi-currency. Often multi-entity. Operating in places where one-by-one onboarding falls apart. The business is moving faster than the financial infrastructure underneath it.

A single bank application no longer covers the operations. The treasury team is stitching together six relationships with different login credentials. The CFO does not know which provider holds which corridor. Time and money leak through the seams.

That is the pattern UQT was built to fix.

Eight archetypes we serve.

01. Global startups

Pre-Series A through growth, expanding from a home market into the EU, UK and MENA. Need operating banking in each market, multi-currency treasury, and a single relationship to manage it.

02. E-commerce and marketplaces

Settlement-heavy operations. High volumes of small transactions in many currencies. Need acquiring relationships, multi-currency operating accounts, and FX rails that price retail flow.

03. Trading firms

Proprietary, market-making and broker-dealer operations. Strict sector-fit requirements at banks. Often need digital-asset custody and OTC alongside fiat banking.

04. Professional services

Law firms, consultancies, agencies. Multi-entity holding and operating structures. Client-money obligations. Need escrow capability and clear segregation.

05. Import / export

Trade-finance flows. Cross-border supplier settlement. Emerging-market currencies. Need banking that understands trade and FX that prices the right corridors.

06. Payment-heavy SaaS

Subscription, marketplace and payout flows in many currencies. Need multi-currency operating banking, API-friendly FX, and good ramps if stablecoin treasury is part of the model.

07. Crypto-native businesses

Regulated digital-asset operations. Need fiat banking that will engage with the activity, regulated custody, and on/off ramps that fit the jurisdiction.

08. Multi-entity groups

Holding companies, operating subsidiaries, special-purpose vehicles. Need one consolidated profile that opens accounts across the group in the right structures.

Who we are not for.

UQT is built for business and operations. We do not work with retail customers seeking personal banking. We do not place clients with unlicensed providers. We do not engage with activity that has no regulated route in the relevant jurisdiction. If we cannot help, we will say so on the first call.
