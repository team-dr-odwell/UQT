About this policy

This Cookies Policy explains how UQT, operated by UQG Technologies L.L.C, uses cookies and similar technologies on www.uqt.ae and the UQT portal (portal.uqt.ae). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site's owners.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to function. Examples include session cookies that keep you signed in to the portal, and cookies that store your cookie-preference choice. These cannot be turned off without affecting how the site works.

Performance and analytics

Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These collect aggregated, anonymous information about pages visited, time spent and approximate location. You can opt out of analytics cookies in the cookie banner.

Functional

Remember choices you make, such as language and region, to provide a more personal experience. Disabling these may degrade some functionality.

Marketing

We do not currently set advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies. If we add any in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Managing cookies

You can change your preferences using the cookie banner that appeared on your first visit. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies altogether, or to delete cookies that have already been set. Note that strictly necessary cookies cannot be refused without breaking the site.

Third-party cookies

When third-party services are embedded on our pages (for example, web fonts or analytics), those services may set their own cookies. We list any third parties we use in our Privacy Policy.

Updates to this policy

If we materially change the cookies we use, we will post the updated policy here.
