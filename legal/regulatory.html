Regulatory disclaimer.

UQT is an international brokerage and technology platform. Regulated financial services are delivered by the independent regulated providers in our network. This disclaimer sets out the position clearly.

UQT is not a regulated financial-service provider

UQT, operated by UQG Technologies L.L.C, is an international brokerage and technology platform. UQT connects businesses to a network of independent, regulated financial-service providers. UQT is not a bank, payment institution, money services business, virtual-asset service provider, broker-dealer or financial broker. UQT does not provide banking, payment, currency-exchange, virtual-asset, custody or brokerage of financial instruments services. UQT does not hold client funds, exchange currency or custody digital assets.

Regulated services in the network

Every regulated financial service in the UQT network is delivered by an independent regulated provider, licensed in its own jurisdiction. The decision to accept any business and to provide any regulated service rests with the provider. UQT routes, packages and monitors. The provider decides and operates.

Licence and jurisdiction

UQT, the trading name of UQG Technologies L.L.C, operates from Dubai and serves clients globally through its brokerage and matching activity. UQT does not hold a banking, payment-services, virtual-asset-services, money-services or securities licence in any jurisdiction.

Information on the Site

Information published on www.uqt.ae and shared during conversations with UQT is for general information only and does not constitute financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Matching results, scored shortlists and partner suggestions are informational outputs of UQT's matching engine and the judgement of our agents, and do not amount to advice or any recommendation as to a specific financial product or transaction.

Provider relationships

Your relationship with any regulated provider that you onboard with through UQT is governed by your agreement with that provider, not by UQT. UQT does not act as principal in any regulated transaction. UQT does not have authority to bind any provider, and providers do not have authority to bind UQT.

Marketing communications

Any marketing material describing the providers in our network is for informational purposes. Where statistics are quoted (such as time saved, acceptance rates or coverage figures) those figures are indicative based on UQT's observation of its own client base and may not represent the results any particular client will experience.
