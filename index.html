What does UQT actually do?

UQT is an international brokerage. We sit between your business and 200+ regulated banking, FX and digital-asset providers, capture your profile once, and route a packaged application to every partner that fits. We do not hold funds, exchange currency or custody digital assets. The providers we connect you to deliver those services.

Is UQT regulated?

UQT is the trading name of UQG Technologies L.L.C, a Dubai mainland limited liability company licensed by the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) under licence number 1624196. The regulated financial services themselves are delivered by the providers in our network, each licensed in their own jurisdiction.

How long does the matching process take?

Profile capture takes under an hour with your agent. The engine produces a scored shortlist the same day. From first call to live accounts, most clients are running in two to four weeks, compared with twelve to twenty if they apply to providers one by one.

What information do you need from us?

Structure, activity, geography, currency mix, expected volumes, key counterparties. Standard incorporation documents and beneficial-owner identification. Your agent walks you through the list. Anything missing, we collect it in the portal.

How do you choose which providers we get matched to?

Every provider in our network is loaded with what they look for. Sectors they accept, transaction sizes, jurisdictions, risk appetite. The engine scores your profile against every one. A senior agent then reviews the shortlist before any introduction goes out. You see why each partner was chosen.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Our consultation is free to business owners. We are paid by the financial institutions we connect you to. Because we introduce thousands of businesses every year, the scale of our network means our clients regularly access better rates than they could secure direct, and they do so in a fraction of the time.
