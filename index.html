Stage 01 / Capture

Tell us about your business.

Structure, activity, geography, currency mix, transaction patterns. We capture the picture of how you operate, not a generic risk template. Your specialist works the file with you in the portal.

Stage 02 / Map

We map you to the network.

Every provider in our 200+ network is loaded with what they look for. Sectors, sizes, jurisdictions, risk appetite. The engine reads your profile against every single one.

Stage 03 / Score

Every provider scored against you.

The engine ranks the partners most likely to approve and serve you, with the reasons behind each score. A senior agent reviews the shortlist before any introduction goes out.

Stage 04 / Route

Introductions packaged and routed.

One application, packaged for every matched provider. You watch status from the portal as accounts open. One relationship instead of many. Weeks back in your calendar.
