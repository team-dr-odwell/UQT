What we route to.

Digital assets are the part of the network where most introductions go wrong. Either the provider is the wrong shape for the activity, or the activity is the wrong shape for the jurisdiction. UQT brokers introductions to 50 regulated digital-asset partners, only where the fit is real.

We do not custody digital assets. We do not run an exchange. We do not hold client funds in crypto. We connect you to regulated partners who do, in jurisdictions where they are permitted to.

Our position on digital assets.

UQT does not market digital assets to retail customers. We do not provide investment advice on them. We do not custody them ourselves. We route businesses with legitimate digital-asset activity to the regulated providers best placed to serve them.

If a client's planned activity does not have a regulated route in their jurisdiction, we say so on the first call. We will not place a client with a partner where the fit is forced.

Compliance posture.

!

Regulated routes only

Every digital-asset partner in our network is either a regulated VASP in its home jurisdiction, or holds an equivalent licence. We do not place clients with unlicensed providers. Compliance, AML and KYC are run by each partner.

Digital-asset FAQs.

Does UQT custody our digital assets?

No. UQT is an introducer. Custody sits with the regulated digital-asset custodian you select from the shortlist.

Will you work with crypto-native businesses?

Yes. We work with crypto-native businesses where regulated routes exist for their activity. If they do not, we will tell you.

Do you cover stablecoin payments?

Yes. Stablecoin treasury and settlement is one of the fastest-growing parts of the network. We route to regulated partners with proper fiat ramps.
