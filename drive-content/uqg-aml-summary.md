KEY POSITIONS (extracts from the manual)

Zero-tolerance policy. The Company enforces a strict AML policy with zero tolerance on ML/TF activities. The AML & CFT Manual sets out the policies and measures implemented by the Company to ensure compliance with the applicable legal framework.

Risk-based approach. The Company applies appropriate measures and procedures by adopting a risk-based approach, so as to focus effort in those areas where the risk of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing appears to be comparatively higher. The approach recognises that ML/TF threat varies across clients, countries, services and financial instruments.

What money laundering is. Money Laundering is a process of concealing the true origin and ownership of illegally obtained money — principally proceeds of criminal activities such as illicit drugs, corruption, organised crime, fraud, sex trade, forgery, illegal logging or fishing, revenue evasion, counterfeit money, piracy and terrorism.

Three stages of money laundering: placement, layering, integration.

Terrorist financing. Provision or gathering of funds by any means, directly or indirectly, with the intention to use such funds or knowing that they will be used in whole or in part for the commission of an offence within the meaning given to the term by section 4 of the International Convention for the Suppression of the Financing of Terrorism (Ratification and Other Provisions) Law and by sections 5 to 13 of the Combating of Terrorism Law.

MLRO and Compliance Officer. In accordance with the provisions of the Law and Directive, the Company appoints a Compliance Officer / MLRO, a senior management official with sufficient skills, experience and knowledge in AML/CFT compliance.

Customer due diligence covers identification, verification, ongoing monitoring, transaction monitoring, client acceptance procedures, record keeping, and suspicious transaction reporting.

Applicable law:
- The Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing law of 2007 L188(I)/2007 as amended.
- Directive (EU) 2015/849 (4AMLD)
- Directive (EU) 2018/843 (5AMLD)
- Directive (EU) 2018/1673
- Sanctions Law (L. 58(I)/2016)
- Terrorism Law (L. 75(I)/2019)

Sources monitored: FATF, MONEYVAL, EU Commission, UN, IMF, OECD, Transparency International, CySEC, Central Bank of Cyprus, EU and UN sanctions lists, EU External Action Service, CFSP.

Penalties. ML offences are punishable by up to 14 years imprisonment and/or a penalty of up to €500,000 where a person "knows" of a money laundering offence, and by 5 years imprisonment and/or a penalty of up to €50,000 where a person "ought to have known". Tipping off carries up to 2 years imprisonment and/or a fine of €50,000. Failure to report carries up to 2 years imprisonment and/or a fine not exceeding €5,000.
